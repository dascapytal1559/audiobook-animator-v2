# A4: Keep original audio unchanged and preserve source-relative timing for every cut.

**Status.** Standing.

**Decision.** Keep original audio unchanged and preserve source-relative timing for every cut. A story is the author's narrative unit, not an arbitrary duration segment.

**Why.** Later transcripts and visuals must remain traceable to the original narration.
