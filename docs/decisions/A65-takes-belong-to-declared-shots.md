# A65: Takes belong to declared shots; a shot is declared by anchoring it to a word, and the story map only navigates.

**Status.** Standing (2026-09-23). Requested by the user after rejecting automatic scene splitting: the map's beats and scenes split at odd places, so nothing automatic decides where a shot begins; the user says explicitly when a new shot starts inside a beat.

**Decision.** A *declared shot* is a place where a new shot begins, declared by anchoring a shot on the visual timeline to a transcript word (A51). The word is the shot's identity: the shot starts at that word's effective start, so it follows timing edits, and the takes of the shot name that word. The declared shots of a story are the distinct anchor words of the shots its image tracks show (`declaredShots` in `packages/domain/src/shots.ts`), in timeline order. A hidden or unselected shot declares nothing, and an unanchored shot is a placement, not a declaration. Nothing splits a beat on its own, and the story map (A62) declares no shot: it is for navigating acts and beats.

A description take names the declared shot it depicts by the shot's anchor word. `scene-descriptions.json` is version 2: `{ schemaVersion: 2, kind: "scene-descriptions", clip, updatedAt, takes }`, each take `{ id, anchorWordId, model, text, prompt?, notes?, createdAt, producer }`. A writer appends one through `POST /api/stories/<id>/scene-descriptions` with `{ anchorWordId, model, text, prompt?, notes? }`; the server refuses a word no declared shot starts at, and a repeat of the same shot, model, and text as `TakeExists`. No map section needs to exist for a take to be recorded or shown. Version 1, which keyed takes to map sections and copied the section's word range, is gone rather than read alongside.

An image take of a declared shot is the image each track starts with it, one per track, labelled with the track as before.

The **Scenes** detail (A63) shows a scene shot by shot, in timeline order: the declared shot holding at the scene's start, marked when it started before the scene, then each declared shot starting inside it. Each is headed by its first spoken words and its start, which seeks there, and shows its image takes and its description takes side by side, none picked. Whatever else a track shows at the scene's start (an unanchored shot, or an earlier shot's image still holding) is listed once, after the holding shot, as no declared shot and with no descriptions, since a description can only name a declared shot. A scene with nothing to show keeps that one empty group, so the pane reads the same before and after generation.

**Why the anchor word and not the start sample.** A sample moves whenever word timing is realigned or corrected by hand, so a take keyed to one would silently detach from its shot, or attach to another, after a timing edit; the word does not move, and the timeline already uses it as the shot's anchor. Keying by the word also keeps the file honest when shots change. A shot added at a new word starts with no takes. A shot moved to another word, or removed by hiding or detaching its images, leaves its takes on the old word, where they no longer show, rather than lending them to a moment they were not written for. The takes stay in the file, since it only grows, and show again if a shot is declared at that word. A separate list of declared shots with their own ids was considered and not built: it would be a second record of what the anchors already say, and moving a shot would carry takes to a passage they were not written for.

**Consequences.** Declaring a shot needs an image on some track anchored to its word, so a description cannot come before any image of its shot. Dragging a shot marker detaches its anchor (A51), so a shot moved by dragging is no longer declared until it is snapped to a word again. The takes of a shot moved to another word stay on the old word. The editor's own server of an earlier build cannot read the version 2 file and reports it as invalid until it runs this build.
