# A12: Keep selection synopses short and focused on the premise, without revealing endings.

**Status.** Standing.

**Decision.** Keep selection synopses short and focused on the premise, without revealing endings. Store editable descriptions separately from completed split evidence, linked by story ID and transcript hash.

**Why.** Helps the user compare stories while allowing descriptions to change without repeating media work or invalidating accepted outputs.
