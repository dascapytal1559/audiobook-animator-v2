# A9: Export story audio as lossless 24-bit FLAC at the source sample rate and channel count, with a matching timed transcript beside it.

**Status.** Standing.

**Decision.** Export story audio as lossless 24-bit FLAC at the source sample rate and channel count, with a matching timed transcript beside it. Extraction requests use integer decoded-audio sample intervals with an exclusive end.

**Why.** Preserve narration quality and reproducible cuts for later movie work; the source MP3 remains unchanged.

**Cited by.** not cited in code.
