# A13: Story plans distinguish facts supported by the transcript, uncertain interpretations, and invented visual details.

**Status.** Standing.

**Decision.** Story plans distinguish facts supported by the transcript, uncertain interpretations, and invented visual details. Preserve raw transcription and attach evidence to interpretations or corrections.

**Why.** Recognition errors and unspecified visual details must not silently become story canon.

**Cited by.** not cited in code.
